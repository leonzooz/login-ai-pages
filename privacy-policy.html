2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Audit domains — the URL you submit for a free audit, and any competitor URLs you submit when purchasing a comparison report.
  • Payment-related information — the email address you provide at checkout. Card data is collected directly by the payment processor and never passes through us.
  • Correspondence — the content of emails you exchange with us.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, operating system
  • IP address (used for rate limiting and abuse prevention)
  • Usage behaviour: time on page, clicks, audit request timestamps
  • Server logs

2.3 Third-party website data retrieved during audits

When running an audit, we issue read-only public requests to the domain you submit (including any competitor domains you specify) and retrieve its homepage, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt. This is publicly available data that anyone can fetch; we do not log in, modify anything, or deep-crawl. The retrieved content is used solely to produce your report.

Payment card data is never stored on our servers. All card information is handled exclusively by our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor, Waffo Pancake.

6. Security Measures

  • All transmission is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS
  • Stored data is access-controlled; only necessary personnel can access it
  • Payment card data never touches our servers
  • Report links use 128-bit random values and are not guessable
  • Access permissions and logs are reviewed periodically

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users and, to the extent legally required, the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of discovery.

10. International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure and providers are distributed across several countries and regions, including the United States (Cloudflare, GitHub, Resend), Japan (Resend's Tokyo sending region) and China (DeepSeek, used only for content quality evaluation in certain deep-analysis features). This means your data may be transferred outside your country for processing.

For these transfers we rely on the safeguards adopted by each provider, such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or equivalent mechanisms. If you do not want your content processed in a particular region, contact us before purchasing and we will confirm whether the service can be provided another way.
